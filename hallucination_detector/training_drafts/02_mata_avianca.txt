The Eleventh Circuit held that equitable tolling applies to claims
under the Montreal Convention. Varghese v. China Southern Airlines,
925 F.3d 1339 (11th Cir. 2019). That holding was reaffirmed in
Shaboon v. EgyptAir, 410 F.3d 1287 (11th Cir. 2014), and again in
Petersen v. Iran Air, 905 F. Supp. 2d 121 (D.D.C. 2012). Plaintiff
respectfully submits that the same rule should apply here.
